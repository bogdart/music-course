# Curriculum Outline — 52 weeks, 2–3 lessons/week

Learner profile: adult; knows music theory on paper (scales, octaves, keys exist) but started with a near-zero ear
(octave same/different at chance after week 1; notes in different octaves sound like different notes) and knew
nothing about chords. Has a small MIDI keyboard; answers by keyboard or clicks only (no microphone).
Target after 12 months: (a) take a musical idea to a finished multi-track song in the DAW, (b) decompose a current
pop song by ear (form, key, chords, bass, melody, groove).

**Revision (September 2026).** A full review found that every ear skill sped up several steps per week with nothing
checking readiness, that concepts were used before being taught, and that the transcription phase showed the answers
on screen. The course is now built on two tracks:

* **Concepts & making** (theory, keyboard, songs, DAW) follow this calendar.
* **Ear training** runs on ten mastery-gated ladders (`docs/EAR_LADDERS.md`): lessons *open* rungs, the learner
  *masters* them; every ear drill runs at the learner's current rung, never at a fixed difficulty.

Rules for every lesson (see also CLAUDE.md "Who this is for"):

1. **Teach before use.** No term, chord, reference sound or skill appears in prose or a drill before a lesson has
   explained it with something to hear. If a later lesson needs an idea early, give it a short proper explanation
   where it is first used.
2. **Honest perception.** Describe what the learner will actually hear at their stage, never an idealised claim
   ("octaves sound the same", "your ear re-centres within a bar").
3. **Load.** At most ~3 genuinely new ideas per lesson; a DAW task must fit its lesson (or say it spans two sessions).
4. **Two before many** in any choice the learner makes; one new dimension at a time.
5. **Transcription is real.** When the task is to hear something, the answer is hidden (`"hidden": true` examples,
   no hints in question text, facts in quiz `explain` revealed after answering: *verdict first, then compare*).
6. Pass scores for fixed exercises: 0.7 by default; reviews and assessments are *diagnostic* (0.7) and only test
   what was drilled.

Weekly rhythm: L1 = theory + ear, L2 = keyboard + ear, L3 = make/apply (DAW or song). Lesson ids: `wNN-lM-slug`.
Ladder rungs opened by each lesson: see the unlock schedule in `docs/EAR_LADDERS.md`.

## Phase 1 — Foundations (weeks 1–8)

**Goal:** find, play and name notes; begin octave hearing (together → which-of-two → one after the other); steps and
the first intervals; the major scale and "home"; degrees 1–7 in C, then G and F; first triads and the cadence; steady
time in 4/4 and 3/4; first melodies and first DAW sketch.

| Wk | Theme | Lessons |
|----|-------|---------|
| 1 | Sound, pitch, the keyboard (5 lessons; may take two calendar weeks) | welcome, app tour, keyboard setup; **higher, lower, the same** (pitch direction and "the same note twice"); **find the note you hear** (the keyboard search: middle first, compare, move, merge); octaves — two properties of a note (height, name), octaves *melt when played together*, honest that they won't sound "the same" yet; first melody by ear (Hot Cross Buns, Mary Had a Little Lamb, 3 notes) |
| 2 | Note names, steps, beat | white keys & landmarks; black keys, sharps/flats, half & whole steps; pulse, tempo, metronome, first DAW: 4 bars of quarter notes |
| 3 | Major scale & home | W-W-H pattern, C major hands-on; scale degrees & solfège, "home" heard first (tunes that stop early), the melodic home run 1-2-3-4-5-4-3-2-1 as the reference; Ode to Joy, DAW melody from degrees 1–5 |
| 4 | Rhythm & notation basics | note values & rests (degree 4, "resolve" = moving to a more restful note, defined with sound); time signatures 4/4 & 3/4, counting, hearing 3 vs 4; reading rhythm & treble clef C4–G5 |
| 5 | Intervals I | 2nds & 3rds (step vs skip, dark vs bright 3rd); 4ths & 5ths with anchor songs; intervals on the staff, drone DAW |
| 6 | Triads & the cadence | major triads: chord, triad, root; *the cadence explained* (home → away → tension → home) and introduced as the ear reference; minor triads, major vs minor by ear; chords of C major & roman numerals (I ii iii IV V vi vii°: diminished explained), degree 6 |
| 7 | Keys G and F | G & F major, key signatures, circle of fifths intro (one new key at a time); transposing a melody; same melody in three keys (DAW) |
| 8 | Phrases & first song | phrases and *phrase-ending* cadences (half vs authentic — distinguished from the "cadence" reference sound), leading tone 7; Phase-1 review (diagnostic, no new material); DAW: first 8-bar song with chords, bass roots, melody, drums |

## Phase 2 — Harmony & Songs (weeks 9–16)

**Goal:** minor keys, the remaining intervals, inversions and hearing the root, seventh chords, functional harmony and
the common pop progressions; first random keys (week 11); bass clef and both hands; first song analyses (verdict
first).

| Wk | Theme | Lessons |
|----|-------|---------|
| 9 | Minor keys | natural minor & relative keys (♭3 ♭6 ♭7 notation and minor numeral case explained); harmonic/melodic minor, v vs V (the raised G♯ explained); Greensleeves & minor DAW (i–VI–III–VII in A minor, upper-case numerals explained) |
| 10 | Intervals II & bass clef | 6ths, 7ths, octave vs 5th (the "fifth trap"); the tritone & descending direction; bass clef C2–C4, left hand, low-register octaves |
| 11 | Inversions & the root | triad inversions & smooth voice leading; hearing the root (bass vs root; first random-key degree drill, explained); hands together, re-voicing I–V–vi–IV |
| 12 | Seventh chords | triad vs 7th, maj7 / dom7 / min7; dominant function V7→I (tension and resolution defined); ii–V–I (predominant defined) & a ballad DAW |
| 13 | Functional harmony & pop progressions | T–S–D families (iii and vii° heard before they are sorted), cadences (authentic, plagal, half, deceptive); the four chords & common progressions; analysing three pop songs (by reference, verdict first) |
| 14 | Rhythm II & groove | 8ths & 16ths, syncopation; triplets, swing and 6/8 (compound meter explained); drum beats in two genres (DAW) |
| 15 | Melody & harmony together | chord tones vs non-chord tones (passing, neighbour, suspension); contour & phrasing, descending intervals in melodies; sus2 & sus4 heard, harmonising a melody (DAW) |
| 16 | Circle of fifths & borrowed chords | full circle, all major keys (D, A, E… built properly); relative/parallel keys, borrowed iv & ♭VII, minor progressions; Phase-2 review (diagnostic) & 16-bar song |

## Phase 3 — Songwriting & Arrangement (weeks 17–26)

**Goal:** write complete songs (form, hook, melody, harmony, bass, drums, layering) and arrange them; understand the
structure of popular songs; dictate simple melodies and bass lines at the learner's ladder level.

| Wk | Theme | Lessons |
|----|-------|---------|
| 17 | Song form | verse/pre/chorus/bridge, intro/outro, common forms, tempo by ear; analysing forms of three pop songs (verdict first); blocking out a form in the DAW |
| 18 | Writing melodies | motif, repetition & variation, question/answer; range, climax, hook; chorus hook from a 2-bar motif (DAW) |
| 19 | Bass lines | roots, 5ths, octaves, approach notes, lock with the kick; bass dictation (minor keys); three bass styles on one progression (DAW) |
| 20 | Drums & groove design | kit vocabulary, fills, ghost notes, velocity; genre grooves (half-time defined); an 8-bar drum arrangement with a fill |
| 21 | Layering & arrangement | frequency roles, doubling (honest: an octave double sounds like a new layer at first), counter-melody, pads vs comping; textural build; arrange 32 bars (two sessions) |
| 22 | Modes I | the ♭7 (Mixolydian) and raised 6 (Dorian), each against its parent scale on the same root; the ♯4 (Lydian) and ♭2 (Phrygian); 8 bars in two modes (DAW) |
| 23 | Pentatonic, blues & riffs | major/minor pentatonic; 12-bar blues, blue notes, shuffle; riff and step-entered solo (DAW) |
| 24 | Secondary dominants & chromaticism | V/V and V/vi (the ♯4 and ♯5 heard first); chromatic passing chords (dim7 and m7♭5 explained); re-harmonise an earlier song |
| 25 | Songwriting workshop | idea → sketch → structure → arrangement; hook-first vs chord-first vs beat-first; writing session with checklist (two sessions) |
| 26 | Phase-3 capstone | two original songs end to end (verse/chorus/bridge, 4+ tracks, levels, pan); listening review & diagnostic ear check |

## Phase 4 — Composition Studio & Jazz (weeks 27–40)

**Goal:** fluent composition across styles; jazz harmony and voicings (by hand and by ear at the learner's level);
improvisation basics; production-minded arrangement; a portfolio of songs.

| Wk | Theme | Lessons |
|----|-------|---------|
| 27 | Extended chords | 9ths, 11ths, 13ths (what to leave out, simply); add9, 6 chords; neo-soul progression (DAW) |
| 28 | Jazz voicings | shell voicings (3-7); rootless voicings A/B (by hand); ii–V–I around the cycle (major) |
| 29 | Jazz standards structure | 32-bar AABA, rhythm changes (ii–V into IV explained); jazz blues; minor ii–V–i; comp a standard-like progression |
| 30 | Borrowed chords & reharmonisation (4 lessons) | borrowed chords incl. ♭VI and the ♭VI–♭VII–I cadence; the augmented chord and the ♭9 idea; tritone substitution & passing diminished; reharmonise a pop progression three ways |
| 31 | Melody over changes | chord-scales, guide tones (locrian explained if used); approach notes & enclosures; an improvised or step-entered chorus |
| 32 | Counterpoint basics (4 lessons) | two-voice writing: consonance/dissonance heard together, parallel 5ths/8ves; first species (cantus firmus, four rules); contrary motion; counter-melody (DAW) |
| 33 | Electronic music production | synthesis (osc, filter, envelope), arps, sidechain feel; EDM 32-bar build/drop |
| 34 | Rhythm III | odd meters (5/4, 7/8); polyrhythm 3:2 (metric modulation optional, heard); a 7/8 groove |
| 35 | Songwriting in genres I | pop, rock (power chords defined), folk (6/8): idioms, progressions, arrangement; one 60-second sketch per genre |
| 36 | Songwriting in genres II | hip-hop/trap, house, lo-fi: beats, layering with synths; one sketch per genre |
| 37 | Film & game cues | mood via mode/tempo/orchestration, ostinati, leitmotif; 60-second cue |
| 38 | Advanced form & development | motif development (inversion, augmentation, fragmentation), through-composed forms; develop a motif for 32 bars |
| 39 | Melody for lyrics (no singing required) | stress, range, prosody; topline writing over a beat; toplines over three beats |
| 40 | Portfolio review | finish and polish three songs over several sessions; self-critique checklist; diagnostic ear check |

## Phase 5 — Transcription & Mastery (weeks 41–52)

**Goal:** decompose real pop songs by ear (form, key, tempo, chords, bass, melody, groove, arrangement) and write
finished songs quickly. Every transcription hides the answer until the learner commits.

| Wk | Theme | Lessons |
|----|-------|---------|
| 41 | Transcription method | workflow: key & tempo → form → bass roots → chord qualities → melody → groove; key and tempo by ear (tap tempo); first hidden mystery song |
| 42 | Hearing the bass | bass in full mixes (example tracks, hidden); root vs inversion in context; three named songs' bass lines (verdict first) |
| 43 | Chord qualities in context | predicting quality from the key; triads vs 7ths inside progressions |
| 44 | Hearing melody | leaps and chromatic notes; melody over harmony; dictation built up 2 → 4 → 8 bars |
| 45 | Groove & arrangement | drum layers one at a time, instruments/layers, section changes; recreate a 32-bar mix (two sessions) |
| 46 | Transcribe 1: pop ballad | full decomposition of an app-generated ballad (hidden) + verdict-first reference analysis of a named song |
| 47 | Transcribe 2: dance/pop | same for an up-tempo track |
| 48 | Transcribe 3: R&B/neo-soul | extended harmony (families and roots), syncopated groove |
| 49 | Transcribe 4: rock/indie | riffs, power chords, Mixolydian/♭VII |
| 50 | Speed songwriting (4 sessions) | idea → finished 2-minute song twice (from a hook, from a beat), each over two sessions |
| 51 | Final project (4 sessions) | one fully finished song, 3+ minutes: brief & core, structure, layers, review |
| 52 | Graduation (3 lessons) | diagnostic ear and theory review of what was drilled; transcription of a hidden song; retrospective & next steps |
