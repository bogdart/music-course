# Curriculum Outline — 52 weeks, 2–3 lessons/week

Learner profile: absolute beginner, zero practical skill, cannot yet hear a
chord root or octave equivalence. Has a small MIDI keyboard (~25–49 keys).
Target after 12 months: (a) take a musical idea to a finished multi-track song
in the DAW, (b) decompose a current pop song by ear (form, key, chords, bass,
melody, groove).

Weekly rhythm: L1 = theory + ear, L2 = keyboard + ear, L3 = make/apply (DAW or
song). Every lesson starts with an automatic SRS review (ear cards).

Lesson ids: `wNN-lM-slug`. Each week has 3 lessons unless marked (2).

---

## Phase 1 — Foundations (weeks 1–8)  → content-1

**Goal:** name, play and hear notes; octave equivalence; steps and intervals up
to the octave; major scale; major/minor triads; steady time in 4/4 and 3/4;
first melodies and first DAW sketch.

| Wk | Theme | Lessons |
|----|-------|---------|
| 1 | Sound, pitch, the keyboard | welcome + app tour + keyboard setup; pitch high/low & octaves (same-or-different octave ear drill, why octaves sound "the same"); first melody by ear (Hot Cross Buns / Mary Had a Little Lamb, 3 notes) |
| 2 | Note names, half/whole steps, beat | white keys & landmark C; black keys, sharps/flats, half & whole steps; pulse, tempo, metronome, first DAW: record 4 bars of quarter notes |
| 3 | Major scale & scale degrees | W-W-H pattern, C major hands-on; scale degrees & solfège (do re mi), ear: degrees 1-2-3 with cadence reference; play "Ode to Joy" RH; DAW: melody from degrees 1-5 |
| 4 | Rhythm & notation basics | note values (w h q 8), rests, ties, dotted; 4/4 & 3/4, counting; reading rhythm & tapping; treble clef note reading C4–G5 |
| 5 | Intervals I | unison/2nds/3rds — ear (M2 vs M3) & play; 4ths/5ths — ear, "Here Comes the Bride"/"Twinkle" reference songs; intervals on staff; DAW: 2-voice exercise (melody + drone) |
| 6 | Triads | major triad build/hear; minor triad build/hear (maj vs min ear drill); triads on each scale degree of C major, roman numerals I ii iii IV V vi vii°; DAW: I–V–vi–IV block chords + melody |
| 7 | Keys & key signatures I | G and F major, sharps/flats in key signatures, circle of fifths intro; transposing a melody; ear: degrees 1–5 in G and F; DAW: same melody in 3 keys |
| 8 | Consolidation & first song | 8-bar song structure (A A'), phrase & cadence (V→I), "Amazing Grace"/"Ode to Joy" full RH+LH root; Phase-1 review + ear assessment (2); DAW: complete first 8-bar song: chords, bass roots, melody, drums |

## Phase 2 — Harmony & Songs (weeks 9–16)  → content-2

**Goal:** minor keys, all intervals incl. compound & harmonic, 7th chords,
inversions, functional harmony, common pop progressions; hear bass lines and
chord roots reliably; play both hands; read bass clef; first analyses of real
pop songs (by reference).

| Wk | Theme | Lessons |
|----|-------|---------|
| 9 | Minor keys | natural minor & relative minor; harmonic/melodic minor, V in minor; ear: major vs minor scales & keys; "Greensleeves"; DAW: minor progression i–VI–III–VII |
| 10 | Intervals II & bass clef | 6ths/7ths/tritone ear & play; harmonic intervals (both notes together) ear; bass clef reading C2–C4, LH; DAW: bass line on roots + melody |
| 11 | Inversions & voice leading | triad inversions, smooth voice leading (common tones); ear: chord root when inverted (the core "hear the root" drill); LH chords + RH melody; DAW: re-voice I–V–vi–IV smoothly |
| 12 | Seventh chords | maj7, dom7, min7 build & hear; V7→I, dominant function, tension/resolution; ii–V–I in major; DAW: pop ballad progression with 7ths |
| 13 | Functional harmony & pop progressions | T–S–D functions, cadences (authentic, plagal, half, deceptive); the "four chords" & other common progressions (I–V–vi–IV, vi–IV–I–V, I–vi–IV–V, I–IV–V); ear: 4-chord progressions in major; analysis of 3 named pop songs (form + progression, by reference) |
| 14 | Rhythm II & groove | 8ths & 16ths, syncopation, swing; drum kit & basic beats (rock, pop, hip-hop, house); ear: rhythm dictation; DAW: build a beat + bass + chords in 3 genres |
| 15 | Melody & harmony together | chord tones vs non-chord tones (passing, neighbour, suspension); melodic contour and phrasing; harmonising a given melody; DAW: harmonise "Twinkle" and an original melody |
| 16 | Circle of fifths & modulation basics | full circle, all major keys; relative/parallel keys, borrowed iv & bVII; Phase-2 review + ear assessment (2); DAW: 16-bar song with verse/chorus in two related keys or with borrowed chord |

## Phase 3 — Songwriting & Arrangement (weeks 17–26)  → content-3

**Goal:** write complete songs (form, hook, melody, harmony, bass, drums,
layering), arrange for several instruments, understand song structure of
popular music, transcribe simple melodies and bass lines by ear.

| Wk | Theme | Lessons |
|----|-------|---------|
| 17 | Song form | verse/pre/chorus/bridge, intro/outro, common forms (AABA, VCVCBC); analysing 3 pop songs' forms (by reference); DAW: block out a form with markers & chord sketches |
| 18 | Writing melodies | motif, repetition & variation, question/answer phrases; range, climax, hook; ear: melodic dictation 1-octave in major; DAW: chorus hook from a 2-bar motif |
| 19 | Bass lines | roots, 5ths, octaves, walking approach, rhythmic lock with kick; ear: bass-line dictation over pop progressions; DAW: 3 bass styles on one progression |
| 20 | Drums & groove design | kick/snare/hat vocabulary, fills, ghost notes, humanising velocity; genre grooves (pop, rock, funk, EDM, trap, bossa); DAW: groove library, 8-bar drum arrangement with fill |
| 21 | Layering & arrangement | frequency roles (bass/mid/top), doubling, counter-melody, pads vs rhythmic comping; textural build (add/remove layers across sections); DAW: arrange a 32-bar song with dynamics |
| 22 | Modes | dorian, mixolydian, lydian, phrygian: sound, characteristic tones, songs that use them (by reference); ear: mode identification; DAW: 8 bars in dorian & mixolydian |
| 23 | Pentatonic, blues & riffs | major/minor pentatonic, blues scale, 12-bar blues, riff writing; ear: pentatonic melodies dictation; DAW: 12-bar blues with riff and improvised solo (record) |
| 24 | Secondary dominants & chromaticism | V/V, V/vi, V/IV; chromatic passing chords; ear: recognising secondary dominants; DAW: re-harmonise an earlier song |
| 25 | Lyrics-free songwriting workshop | idea → sketch → structure → arrangement workflow; hook-first vs chord-first vs beat-first; writing session with checklist (2 lessons) |
| 26 | Phase-3 capstone | complete 2 original songs end-to-end (verse/chorus/bridge, 4+ tracks, mixing basics: levels, pan); listening review + ear assessment |

## Phase 4 — Composition Studio & Jazz (weeks 27–40)  → content-4

**Goal:** fluent composition across styles; jazz harmony and voicings;
improvisation basics; production-minded arrangement; a portfolio of songs.

| Wk | Theme | Lessons |
|----|-------|---------|
| 27 | Extended chords | 9ths, 11ths, 13ths, add9, 6 chords; ear: maj7/min7/dom7/m7b5 recognition; DAW: neo-soul/R&B progression |
| 28 | Jazz voicings I | shell voicings (3-7), rootless voicings, LH comping; ii–V–I in all 12 keys (cycle); ear: ii–V–I recognition |
| 29 | Jazz standards structure | 32-bar AABA, rhythm changes, blues in jazz; public-domain standards ("Autumn Leaves"-style analysis by reference, PD tunes played); DAW: comp a standard-like progression |
| 30 | Modal interchange & reharmonisation | borrowed chords, tritone substitution, passing diminished; ear: identifying borrowed chords; DAW: reharmonise a pop progression 3 ways |
| 31 | Melody over changes | chord-scale relationships, guide tones, approach notes; improvisation on ii–V–I; DAW: record improvised chorus |
| 32 | Counterpoint basics | two-voice species-style writing, contrary motion, avoiding parallels; DAW: counter-melody for a chorus |
| 33 | Electronic music production | synthesis basics (osc, filter, envelope) with the app's synths, arps, sidechain feel via velocity/gating; DAW: EDM 32-bar build/drop |
| 34 | Rhythm III | odd meters (5/4, 7/8), polyrhythm 3:2, metric modulation; ear: meter identification; DAW: a 7/8 groove |
| 35 | Songwriting in genres I | pop, rock, folk: idioms, progressions, arrangement conventions; write one 60-second sketch per genre |
| 36 | Songwriting in genres II | hip-hop/trap, house, lo-fi: beats, sampling-style layering with synths; write one sketch per genre |
| 37 | Film & game cues | mood via mode/tempo/orchestration, ostinati, leitmotif; DAW: 60-second cue for a given scene |
| 38 | Advanced form & development | motif development techniques (inversion, augmentation, fragmentation), through-composed forms; DAW: develop a motif for 32 bars |
| 39 | Voice & lyric awareness (no singing required) | melody for a lyric line: stress, range, prosody; topline writing over a beat; DAW: topline over 3 provided beats |
| 40 | Portfolio review | finish and polish 3 songs; self-critique checklist; ear assessment |

## Phase 5 — Transcription & Mastery (weeks 41–52)  → content-5

**Goal:** decompose real pop songs by ear (form, key, tempo, chords, bass,
melody, groove, arrangement) and write finished songs quickly and reliably.

| Wk | Theme | Lessons |
|----|-------|---------|
| 41 | Transcription method | workflow: find key & tempo → form → bass roots → chord qualities → melody → groove; tools in the app (loop, slow-down of your own sketch); practice on app-generated "mystery songs" |
| 42 | Hearing the bass | bass dictation over full mixes (app-generated), root vs inversion; 3 named songs' bass lines described (by reference) |
| 43 | Hearing chord qualities in context | maj/min/7/sus/add9 within progressions; ear: progressions with 7ths & borrowed chords in 4 keys |
| 44 | Hearing melody | melodic dictation with leaps, chromatic notes, over harmony; 8-bar dictation of app-generated pop melodies |
| 45 | Hearing groove & arrangement | drum pattern dictation, identifying instruments/layers, section changes; DAW: recreate an app-generated 32-bar mix |
| 46 | Transcribe 1: pop ballad | full decomposition of an app-generated ballad + guided reference analysis of a named song |
| 47 | Transcribe 2: dance/pop | same for an up-tempo track |
| 48 | Transcribe 3: R&B/neo-soul | extended harmony, syncopated groove |
| 49 | Transcribe 4: rock/indie | riffs, power chords, mixolydian/bVII harmony |
| 50 | Speed songwriting | 3 sessions: idea → finished 2-minute song in one lesson each, different starting points |
| 51 | Final project | one fully finished song, 3+ minutes, full arrangement (2 lessons + review) |
| 52 | Graduation | full ear assessment, theory assessment, transcription test; retrospective & next steps (2) |
