# Capstone Song One: Verse, Chorus, Bridge

This is the song that proves Phase 3: a complete, original, multi-track song with a **bridge**, finished and mixed. Plan on this lesson plus one or two extra sessions — that is normal for a real song.

## The brief

- Form: **I4 V8 PC4 C8 V8 PC4 C8 B8 C8 O4** — 64 bars, about 2.5 minutes at 100 BPM.
- At least 4 tracks: drums, bass, chords, lead (the template gives you 6).
- Use at least **one Phase 3 colour**: a secondary dominant, a passing diminished chord, a borrowed chord, a counter-melody or a modal melody.

You may start from your own sketch (rebuild this form in its key) or from the **template** below: F major, drums pre-programmed for every section, and a suggested chord plan on the pad. Replace any chord you like.

| Section | Bars | Chords (suggestion) | Drums in template |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intro | 1–4 | F C Dm Bb | hats |
| Verse | 5–12, 25–32 | Dm Bb F C ×2 | light (verse 1), groove (verse 2) |
| Pre-chorus | 13–16, 33–36 | Bb C Dm C | groove + snare build |
| Chorus | 17–24, 37–44, 53–60 | F C Dm Bb ×2 | full, crash, fills |
| Bridge | 45–52 | Gm Bb Dm C Gm Bb C C | half-time + build |
| Outro | 61–64 | Bb C F | half-time, final hit |

## Mixing basics: levels and pan

A [[mix]] makes every part audible. Two tools are enough for now, both on each track's mixer strip:

**Levels (volume), in this order:**
1. Mute everything except kick and bass. Balance them until they feel like one instrument.
2. Add snare (about as loud as the kick) and hats (clearly quieter).
3. Add the lead: the loudest melodic part — every note must be clear.
4. Add chords (pad, epiano) *under* the lead. Pull them down until the lead feels comfortable, then a little more.
5. Extras (strings, counter-melody) last and lowest: you should miss them when muted, not notice them when playing.

**[[Pan]]:** keep kick, snare, bass and lead in the centre. Spread the rest: epiano about 30% left, strings about 30% right, hats slightly right. Listen at low volume — if the lead disappears, it's too quiet.

## The finishing checklist

- [ ] The hook appears at least 3 times, and the chorus starts with it or builds to it.
- [ ] Verse and chorus differ in at least two of: register, rhythm, harmony, texture.
- [ ] The highest melody note is in the chorus (or the bridge's peak leads into it).
- [ ] Bass plays the chord root on beat 1 of each chord (or locks with the kick in dance styles).
- [ ] Every section changes at least one layer; the last chorus is the biggest moment.
- [ ] There is a fill, build or drop before each chorus.
- [ ] The bridge brings something new: chords, rhythm, texture or register.
- [ ] The ending is clear: a cadence to I or a planned fade.
- [ ] Mix: lead always audible, kick and bass balanced, nothing clipping, parts panned.
